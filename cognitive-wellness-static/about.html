My Approach

I chose to work in private practice so that I can provide exceptional care to each of my clients. My evaluations are 60 minutes in length so that I can spend enough time learning each person's history, core issues, and overall mental health goals. From this we work together to construct a plan to achieve long-term mental health wellness.
